## Concept map labels (as printed on it; north is up)

The Elden Tree (Ancient Forest) top-left. Frosthold Fortress (Snow Mountains) top-right. The Castle Town (Royal Capital) centre-north. The Skull City (Forbidden Zone) east, between the snow and the desert. The Sands of Elysium (Desert Region) south-east. Rusty Village (Starting Area) west/south-west. The Harbor City (Trade Port) south.

This is a quick read of the picture; a reviewer does the detailed read in the design step.

## Differences from the current map to settle in the design step

- The Elden Tree glows on the concept map. The elf valley must have NO glow (your rule), so the tree stays natural unless you say otherwise.
- Starting area: the concept puts Rusty Village in the west/south-west; the current start is Town 1 at the centre (0,0) and the rusty desert town is in the south-east (1406,1244).
- Skull: the concept has a lava "Skull City" region on the east coast; the current skull HQ is in Town 1 at (0,-402).
- Capital: the concept shows it ringed by a river; the current capital (walls x +-490, z -1934..-1237) has no river around it.
- Coast and desert: the concept has a cliff-edged coast and a ring-shaped temple with an oasis in the desert; the current coast is softer and the badlands have mesas but no temple.
