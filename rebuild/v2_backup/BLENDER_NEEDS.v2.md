# What I need from Blender later

None of these block the map. They are upgrades I could not do in Studio alone.

1. **Skull HQ re-export with separate colour pieces.** The skull is 4 pieces that all share one colour per piece, so the horns, teeth and eye sockets cannot be coloured separately. Re-export it with the export_v2 colour split (bone, darker horn, dark socket insides, teeth) so it stops looking plain white.
2. **Skull HQ with a hollow inside and an open jaw.** Your Town 1 task was to make the jaw the only way in. The current mesh is solid, so nobody can enter. It needs a hollow interior shell and a real opening in the jaw.
3. **Enterable building shells.** The church, cathedral, tavern, lodge and elf shrine are built from Studio parts. They work, but Blender-made versions with real wall thickness, arches and roof detail would look better. The house meshes are solid boxes, so they can never be entered.
4. **Curved road pieces that fit the terrain roads.** The roads are terrain cobblestone now. Edge kerbs or rut decals made to fit the terrain would add detail.
5. **Mountain rock faces.** The cliff behind the alpine village is the old low-poly mountain. Rock meshes (A62-A64 at a larger scale, or new cliff models) would break up the flat face.
6. **More house variety for the capital.** The capital uses 9 house models more than 500 times. 4-6 more townhouse and shop designs would stop the streets repeating.
7. **Bridges for the town rivers.** Nothing needs them yet, but if new roads cross the rivers I'll need A12 bridge variants sized for 20-stud roads.

Still missing, and not something Blender can make: animals, NPC characters, skybox images, sounds and music.
