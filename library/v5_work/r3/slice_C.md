## Repeating patterns
1. **Sibling clones** — rock/building/tree pairs and trios (DS06 canyon walls, CO02 sea stacks, VG02 oaks, VG07 boardwalks, RD03/RD04 waystation-trading posts) differ mainly in colour or scale, not shape/arrangement.
2. **Odd green caps on sea rock** — all three CO02 sea stacks carry a small grass-green top that reads wrong on a bare ocean spire.
3. **Floating / loose bits** — detached shards and hanging strands (VC01_03, VG05_01) and stray ground debris (DS01_01).
4. **Open or gapped geometry on small props** — TW02 street-life items have hollow open tops and black slit gaps (TW02_04, TW02_07).
