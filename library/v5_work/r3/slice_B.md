## Repeating patterns
1. **Grey default-look rock/stone** is the dominant defect: every natural-rock piece (LM24 pool rocks, HM03 tunnel-portal masses, HM02 bridge abutments, FR02 forest ruins, HM08_01 cairn) renders as flat default grey with no colour or surface relief — reads as unfinished blockout. All the colourful built pieces (wizard, monastery) are fine, so the rock/stone material pass was simply never applied.
2. **Open-back dark slabs** on the four WT03/WT04 bookshelves — realistic but visually flat from behind.
3. **Thin sibling variety**: HM01 rope bridges (x3) and HM07 prayer-flag strings (x3) differ only in length; WT03/WT04 bookshelves and FR02 ruins are close variants. Acceptable but borderline.
4. **Stray bright/stripe artifacts**: HM03_02 opening stripes and FR02_04 gold candle tip are the only two that cross into MAJOR.
