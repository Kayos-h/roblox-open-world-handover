## Repeating patterns
1. **Stacked-slab rock = striped layers (worst defect).** LM06_01 and LM23_01 build rock/cliff masses from many thin flat slabs, which render as overlapping horizontal stripes; LM23_01 also has square camouflage colour patches. Both need rebuilding as chunky faceted stone.
2. **Near-default grey, under-coloured rock/ruin surfaces.** LM05_01, LM20_01, LM20_03, LM24_01 read as plain grey with little relief — add stone colour variation.
3. **Open/hollow tops on wall-walk pieces.** CP02_01, CP02_03, CP07_01 show empty open boxes from behind/above; cap or floor them.
4. **Gold star emblems on capital pieces.** CP15_01 (order HQ gable) and CP17_01 (plaza fountain finial) carry prominent gold stars — originality/emblem watch in a Black Clover world; keep matte and generic, not a squad badge.
5. **Sibling variety.** The two sand obelisks (LM14_01/02) differ only trivially.

## Originality
The whole set is themed on the anime **Black Clover** (reference map title). No model copies a named castle/character/creature. Two models carry generic **gold star emblems** (CP15_01 order HQ, CP17_01 plaza fountain) — a star-grade/squad-emblem motif adjacent to Black Clover's Magic Knight symbology. Plain five-point stars are generic, so these are flagged as watch-items, not confirmed copies; keep them matte and understated. No four-leaf-clover emblems, grimoires or HQ copies seen.
