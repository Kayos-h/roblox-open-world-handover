## Repeating patterns
1. **Sibling clones with only colour/minor change**: VC01_04/06, FL02_01/02/03, EV02_01/02 differ too little in shape and arrangement.
2. **Plain grey default or flat single-tone surfaces** with no relief: VC01 rocks, FL02 undersides, RW01_02, NE01_03, NE02_03, EV03 path backs.
3. **Hollow/open-bottom or empty-cavity geometry**: EV03_01 (see-through under steps), TW03_02 (open bowl reads as a hole).
4. **Thin parts reading as detached/fragile**: HB01_02 deck posts, EV02 rope bridges, TW03_03 banner.
5. **Blank back faces**: EV03_02 and others detailed only on the front.

## Originality
No model in this slice copies a recognisable franchise. The elf houses, signs, skull-avenue braziers and world-tree roots are generic fantasy forms.
