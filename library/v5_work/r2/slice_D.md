2. **Cave-mouth siblings are near-identical** — FR03_04/05/06 share the same stacked-slab shape and flat tan colour; a variety defect.
3. **Ruin-tower-stump siblings** — LM29_01 and LM29_02 are both near-featureless cylinders differing mainly by colour.
4. **Blank flat panels** — NE01_04 sign face and WT03_01 bookshelf back are large single-colour slabs that read as empty.
