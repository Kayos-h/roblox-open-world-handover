## Repeating patterns
1. **"Desert ruin block" siblings (SD02_02/04/05)** are all the same recipe: a few plain tan boxes stacked on a visible flat base slab, with almost no ruin detail — they need broken edges, fallen blocks or carved relief and to lose the base slab.
2. **Flat-tan / flat-grey rock masses with no tonal variation** (LM19_04, LM19_05, HL07_01) read as one solid colour; a little tone variation would fix them.
3. **Overlapping-face dark stripes/banding on back faces** (DS08_03, HM06_02, CP18_02) — a recurring construction artifact on the rear of modular pieces.
4. **"Ring temple segment" (LM16_01 and LM16_02)** share the SAME major defect — a blank slab with columns bunched to one side and stubby fins hanging under the base; both need rebuilding, and they do not differ meaningfully from each other.
5. **Big blank slabs reading as untextured default** (WT13_01, LM16_01/02) — large flat faces with no relief look like placeholder geometry.
