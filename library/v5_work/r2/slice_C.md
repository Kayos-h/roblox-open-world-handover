## Repeating patterns
1. **Glow/light-panel reads**: flat bright-white or cyan slabs reading as lit panels (WT13_03, CP08_01, LM23_03) — the single most serious pattern; violates the no-glow rule.
2. **Loose/floating base rubble** on ruins and cliff galleries (LM26_02, CO04_01, LM25_01, HM05_01/02, LM04_02) — fragments not welded to the mass.
3. **Flat single-colour slabs without relief** (RD02_03 awning, NE01_01 sign, EV03_03 path, WT13_03).
4. **Near-black facets** reading as dark holes on light pieces (HL03_02 ice, TW03_01 brazier).
5. **Sibling clones** differing mainly in height/colour (SP01_05 vs 06, HM05_01 vs 02).
