## Repeating patterns
- **Dark/black recessed interiors**: LM07_01 (true inverted-face bug) and LM12_02 (self-shadow in a deep stair slot) both go black in the slot — check normals and recess depth on stair/entrance pieces.
- **Entrance/opening silhouettes too weak**: DS08_01 and NE02_02 fail to communicate "entrance" / "gateway" — openings and part separation need to be bolder for distance reading.
- **Slender/fragile verticals**: SP01_04 spire and DS04_03 palm trunk both read too thin; a bit more mass would help at 30–60 studs.
- **Flat/sparse modular pieces**: EV03_04 path and RD06_05 frame are usable but under-detailed versus neighbours.

No concern. The world is explicitly the project's Black-Clover-themed fantasy setting, but none of these 30 models copy a recognisable specific franchise emblem, character or named building.
