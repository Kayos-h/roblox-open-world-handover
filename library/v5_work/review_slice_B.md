## Systemic issues
1. **Blocky square two-tone colour patches on stone walls** (CP12_01, CP18_01/03/04): the single worst recurring defect, exactly the camo-patch flaw the brief bans. Fix: paint retaining/quay walls one stone colour and express variation with geometry (courses, chamfers, bollards), not random square recolours.
2. **Flat bright-green cap strips on retaining walls** (CP12_01, CP12_02) read as grass slabs, not stone coping. Fix: recolour copings to stone.
3. **Small detached/floating parts on otherwise good castle pieces** (CP02_02 window-bars, CP05_02 roof posts, CP06 banners, CP11_01 gable, CP14_04 rail, CP15_03 brown box): a cleanup pass to snap or weld loose add-ons would clear most MINOR/MAJOR hits here.
4. **Near-clone wall siblings** (CP01_02 vs 01/03): vary the modular straight-wall set so runs do not look repeated.
