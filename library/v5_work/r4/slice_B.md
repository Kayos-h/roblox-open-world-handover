## Repeating patterns
1. **Loose/detached fragments at bases** (LM02_01, RW01_01, SD01_03, mildly HL03_02): stray slabs/planks/rocks splay out instead of sitting cleanly on the ground -- the single most common flaw.
2. **Plain, under-detailed rear faces** (LM12_02, HM09_03): fronts are detailed, backs are flat slabs with stripes and little relief.
3. **Black/see-through gaps & glossy black panels** (LM08_01 deck, HM04_02 panels, WT13_03 stair slots): dark voids that read as holes or light panels rather than shadow.
4. **Sibling variety thin on palms** (DS04_01/02/03): differ mostly by height, not shape; DS04_03 is the most distinct.

## Originality
No originality concerns. No Black Clover motifs (no four-leaf clover emblem, grimoire, or Magic Knight HQ); CP08 water_gate and LM30 ruined castle are generic fantasy forms. WT06_01 floor medallion is a plain compass-star rosette, not the clover.
