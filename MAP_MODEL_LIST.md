# Black Clover game: Phase 1 map model list

Status: PLANNING ONLY. Nothing built yet. All models made by Kiro in Blender (no online/Toolbox models).
Map: 7000 x 7000 studs, StreamingEnabled. Town 1 (big spawn town) centre ~(0, -108), ~900 x 900 studs.
The NE mountain where the wizard tower was meant to go stays as it is. Wizard tower and dungeons are NOT in this phase.

Total unique models: **~329** (each variant counted as its own model; instances placed on the map will be thousands).

---

## Suggested layout on the 7000 x 7000 map

| Area | Biome | Rough centre (X, Z) | Footprint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Town 1 (done) | Central plains | (0, -108) | 900 x 900 |
| Royal capital (city + Wizard King's castle) | Raised plateau, north | (0, -2500) | 1600 x 1600 (biggest area on the map) |
| Rusty town + church | Dry/scorched edge, south-east | (+1900, +1700) | 600 x 600 |
| Alpine village | Highland hills, east (below the tower mountain) | (+2500, -500) | 700 x 700 |
| Elf jungle city | Deep forest, west | (-2400, -600) | 900 x 900 |
| Harbour | South coast | (-600, +2700) | 800 x 800 |

Main roads run from Town 1 to every area, plus a ring road linking neighbours. Rivers cross the roads at bridges.

## NOT AVAILABLE — Kayos-h to source
Things I can't make well as static Blender models, or that are outside a "model":
- **Animals** (horses for stables/carriage, goats, chickens, seagulls, crows, fish). They need rigging and animation.
- **NPC characters** (guards, villagers, elves, the Wizard King).
- **Skybox** images (day / sunset / night).
- **Sounds** (ambience, bells, waves, birds, wind).
- **Photo-quality tileable textures**, if you want them beyond what I can paint and bake in Blender.

Models I discover I need while building get added here, not faked.

## Rules for the build
- Build the shared kit first, since every area uses it.
- Then one area at a time. Test each new model alone with a Blender render before placing it across the map.
- Roblox limit: 20,000 triangles per mesh. Big buildings get split into pieces.
- Small props stay low-poly enough for a 7000-stud streamed map.
