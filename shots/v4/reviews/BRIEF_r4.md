Foam: strips are now wide and 70-90 percent transparent (no bright white tape). 27 islets and sea stacks got solid rock plinths down to the sea floor (they hovered before). A dark-blue far-sea ring now extends the ocean to 13000 studs (the straight horizon seam).

4. Roads: main roads are painted 24 studs wide in warm limestone, trails and ring roads 12 studs wide in dirt. Trees on roads were removed. Castle: banners thinned, training yard fence is dark wood, the pink fence, garden strays and striped stalls were removed from the yard. Church window panes are amber instead of green. Snow terrain colour shaded (222,230,240). Ice lake has Glacier-material mottling. Harbour plaza at (-420,1560) is cobblestone. Mesas tilted, sunk and tinted for variety (44).
